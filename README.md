### Inventory & Sales risk dashboard
Excel-based retail analytics dashboard analyzing SKU inventory, sales-at-risk, stockout risk, and category performance for CPG account support.
**Description:**  
Built an Excel-based retail account analytics dashboard to evaluate SKU-level inventory availability, projected stockout risk, and sales-at-risk across a CPG portfolio. The project converts raw sales, demand, inventory, and replenishment data into an executive-ready view for account reviews and commercial action planning.

**Key skills used:**  
Excel, Power Pivot, Retail Analytics, Inventory Analysis, Sales-at-Risk Modeling

**The problem:**  
A CPG retail account team needs to identify which SKUs are at risk of stockout, quantify the potential sales impact, and prioritize replenishment actions before product availability affects retail performance.

**My insights:**  
- The portfolio includes **1,558 SKUs** with a total inventory value of approximately **$837K**.
- The dashboard identified **44 SKUs at stockout risk in April**, creating a total estimated **sales-at-risk exposure of $399K**.
- Sales at risk accelerates month over month, growing from approximately **$6.7K in Nov-2025** to **$173K in Apr-2026**, showing that availability risk becomes more critical over time.
- Risk is highly concentrated in a few categories: **Household Care, Personal Care, and Beverages** represent the largest share of total sales-at-risk.
- The top-risk SKUs are not only low-coverage items, but also high-value products, making them immediate priorities for account and replenishment teams.

**My recommendations:**  
- Prioritize **P1 Executive Escalation SKUs** first, especially the top 10 items with the highest sales-at-risk exposure.
- Align weekly with demand planning, supply chain, and account teams to validate inbound inventory and replenishment timing.
- Focus category reviews on **Household Care, Personal Care, and Beverages**, since these categories carry the highest commercial risk.
- Use projected ending stock and monthly demand trends to detect future stockouts earlier, not only current inventory shortages.
- Build a recurring account review process using this dashboard to track availability, sales-at-risk, and replenishment actions by SKU and category.
